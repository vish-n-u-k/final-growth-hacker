GrowJin · Dashboard checklist

Five ways to say “do this on your site, then tell us”

Each concept below works. Click through, try the buttons, and compare against the current design in the first tab. The sample data is a made-up coffee shop’s SEO module.

What people expect

The checkbox looks like a button. Click it, and GrowJin fixes the thing.

What actually happens

It only records that they already made the change on their own site. Nothing on the site changes.

Side by side

ConceptHow it removes the confusionBuild effortTrade-off
A · Status labelThe left column is a read-only status. The only buttons say what they do.SmallLeast change. Still a long list.
B · Fix trackerShows the full path: Found → Fixing → Verified. Done is the end of a journey.MediumAdds a “fixing” state to store.
C · One at a timeA single card with two numbered steps. Confirming is clearly step 2.MediumHides the full list behind “up next”.
D · Health reportNo checklist. Checks turn green when the scan sees the fix.LargeDepends on re-scans being fast and reliable.
E · To fix / DoneMoving a card across columns reads as filing it, not triggering it.MediumTwo columns get cramped on phones.
My pick

Ship A now, aim for D. A is a small change in AllModulesDashboard.tsx and fixes the misread immediately. D matches what the product already does (AI-verified checks), so the user’s job becomes “make the score go up” and the tick stops being something they press. B’s step labels are worth borrowing into either one.
