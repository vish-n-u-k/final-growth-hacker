## 6. Ad Campaign Toolkit

### 6.1 Campaign Strategy Overview

**Run campaigns in this order:**
1. **Week 1** — Campaign #1: Awareness (cold audience — never heard of the brand)
2. **Week 2** — Campaign #2: Consideration (warm audience — engaged with content or visited site)
3. **Week 3** — Campaign #3: Conversion / Retargeting (hot audience — visited site, didn't convert)

**Budget split:** 60% Awareness / 25% Consideration / 15% Retargeting

---

### 6.2 Campaign #1 — Awareness (Cold Audience)

**Goal:** Introduce the brand and product to people who have never seen it  
**Platform:** Instagram Reels + Facebook Reels  
**Objective:** Video Views or Reach  
**Budget:** 60% of monthly ad spend

✓ [Benefit 1]
✓ [Benefit 2]
✓ [Benefit 3]
✓ [Free offer / no risk statement]

[CTA → URL]

**Headline:** `[Clear, specific benefit — "Do [X] in [Y] seconds/minutes"]`

**Description:** `[Secondary benefit. Free plan / trial available.]`

**CTA button:** `[Try for Free / Learn More / Sign Up / Get Started]`

- Length: 30–60 seconds, vertical (9:16)

### 6.4 Campaign #3 — Conversion / Retargeting (Hot Audience)

**Goal:** Convert people who visited the site but didn't take action  
**Platform:** Instagram + Facebook  
**Objective:** Conversions — [Free trial / Purchase / Lead form]  
**Budget:** 15% of monthly ad spend  
**Audience:** Website visitors (last 30 days) — exclude people who already converted  
**Frequency cap:** Max 3x per week per person  
**Test strategy:** Run all 3 copy versions for 7 days simultaneously. Keep the best performer.

#### Copy Version A — Urgency / Low friction
```
[Acknowledge they visited — 1 sentence.]

[Reassure them — remove the biggest friction point (price, complexity, risk).]

Here's what you get [with the free plan / right now]:
✓ [Feature 1]
✓ [Feature 2]
✓ [Feature 3]
✓ [No-risk statement]

[CTA → URL]
```

#### Copy Version B — Social Proof
```
"[Real customer quote — pulled from an actual review]" — [Source, Date]

[X] [customers / users / businesses] already [use / trust / rely on] [Brand Name] to [core benefit].

[Low-risk CTA — "costs nothing to try", "takes 2 minutes to set up".]

[CTA → URL]
```

#### Copy Version C — Objection Removal
```
[Acknowledge the hesitation — "We know what you're thinking."]

[State the most common objection — in their words.]

[Answer it directly and specifically.]

Here's exactly what happens when you [sign up / buy / book]:
1. [Step 1 — simple and fast]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]
4. [Step 4 — result / outcome]

[No-risk close — "Free to start / Money-back guarantee / Cancel anytime."]

[URL]
```

### 6.9 Key Performance Metrics (KPIs)

| Metric | Target | Action if Below Target |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per click — India | ₹5–20 | Fix creative or narrow audience |
| Cost per click — USA/UK | $0.50–$2.50 | Fix creative or narrow audience |
| Click-through rate (CTR) | 1.5–3% for video ads | Rewrite or reshoot first 3 seconds |
| Cost per lead / signup | [Set based on LTV] | Optimise landing page |
| Video view rate (50%+) | 20–35% | Hook in first 3 seconds is failing |
| Free-to-paid conversion | 5–15% (SaaS benchmark) | Improve onboarding / email sequence |
| Retargeting CTR | 2–5x higher than cold CTR | Tighten audience to last 14 days |
| Return on ad spend (ROAS) | 2x minimum to continue | Pause, revise creative, retest |

**Review schedule:**
- Every 7 days: Kill any ad with CTR below 0.8% after 1,000 impressions
- Every 14 days: Evaluate cost per conversion. Scale winners by 20% budget increase
- Every 30 days: Full campaign audit. Refresh creatives. Update audiences

### B. Content Idea Swipe File

**Educational content (highest organic reach):**
- "[X] mistakes [your audience] makes with [topic]"
- "How to [achieve result] in [time] without [pain point]"
- "[X] things you should do before [action]"
- "The truth about [common misconception in your industry]"
- "A step-by-step guide to [task your audience struggles with]"

**Social proof content (highest conversion):**
- Before and after: show transformation using your product/service
- Customer testimonial graphic: quote + name + star rating
- Case study: story format — problem, solution, result
- User-generated content: repost customer content with permission
- Milestone post: "[X] customers / orders / downloads — thank you"

**Engagement content (highest saves and shares):**
- "[X] content ideas for [audience type] this week"
- "Save this for later: [useful resource or checklist]"
- "[Industry] trends for [year] — what you need to know"
- Poll: "Which do you prefer: [A] or [B]?"
- "Drop your [niche] below and I'll [give personalised tip]"

**Conversion content (direct CTA):**
- Free trial / demo offer post
- Limited-time discount announcement
- "Here's exactly what you get when you [sign up / buy]"
- Objection-handling post: "Still on the fence? Here's why [reason to act]"
- Comparison post: "[Your product] vs [alternative] — honest breakdown"
