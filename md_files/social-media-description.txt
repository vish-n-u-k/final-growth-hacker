# Social Media Analysis & Growth Strategy

Analyze the business's social media presence and provide a comprehensive audit, performance evaluation, and growth strategy.

IMPORTANT:

* Only analyze platforms that are available and active.
* Do NOT include sections for platforms that do not exist or cannot be found.
* If data is unavailable for a specific metric, state "Data Not Available" instead of making assumptions.
* Tailor all recommendations to the business industry, audience, goals, and current social media maturity level.

## 1. Social Media Presence Overview

Identify all active social media platforms, including but not limited to:

* Instagram
* Facebook
* LinkedIn
* X (Twitter)
* YouTube
* TikTok
* Pinterest
* Threads
* Other relevant platforms

For each active platform provide:

* Profile URL
* Followers/Subscribers
* Following count (if relevant)
* Total posts/content published
* Account age (if available)
* Overall platform health score (0-100)

## 2. Profile & Brand Audit

Evaluate:

* Profile photo quality
* Brand consistency
* Bio/About section effectiveness
* Keywords used in profile
* Contact information
* Website links
* Call-to-action effectiveness
* Story highlights (if applicable)
* Cover/banner images
* Profile completeness

Identify:

* Missing elements
* Optimization opportunities
* Branding inconsistencies

Provide a Profile Optimization Score (0-100).

## 3. Audience Analysis

Analyze:

* Follower quality
* Audience relevance

* Community strength
* Audience demographics (if available)

Identify:

* Whether followers align with business goals
* Growth opportunities
* Audience gaps

Provide an Audience Quality Score (0-100).

## 4. Content Performance Analysis

For each platform provide:

* Engagement trends

* Content effectiveness

Provide a Content Performance Score (0-100).

## Output Requirements

* Do not show sections for unavailable platforms.
